        Misconduct Category Matrix

        Use this to classify the seriousness of the case before deciding whether warning, DI, removal, demotion or dismissal should be considered.

        Minor Misconduct

        Behavioral deviations that disrupt administrative order but do not cause direct financial or legal harm.

        • Habitual or excessive tardiness.
        • Violations of office dress code policies.
        • Failing to log out or punch timecards properly.
        • Minor discourtesy toward colleagues.

        Major Misconduct

        Actions that damage operational productivity, compromise security, or violate corporate governance.

        • Disobeying clear, legitimate instructions from a superior.
        • Installing unapproved software or games on corporate computers.
        • Leaving work early or being absent without notice.
        • Sharing inaccurate information outside the organization.

        Gross Misconduct

        Critical breaches that fundamentally undermine the employment relationship and cause direct financial or reputational harm.

        • Theft, fraud, or dishonesty involving corporate properties.
        • Asking for, offering, or accepting bribes or unauthorized financial favors.
        • Physical fights, threats, or harassment on office premises.
        • Being convicted of a criminal offense by a Court of Law.
        • Falsifying or tampering with attendance and business records.
